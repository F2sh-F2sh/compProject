# Chapter 1 — The Sealed Belfry: Solution Guide

Scene: `res://scenes/chapter/chapter_1.tscn` (generated by `tools/build_chapter.gd`).
The tutorial's exit door leads here. The chapter ends when Echidna rings the great bell in
Room 9: a short ceremony, then the Chapter Complete card (time, deaths, resets, hints,
pages) and back to the title screen.

Nine rooms in four acts: **I The Toll** (R1–R2), **II The Flame** (R3–R4), **III The
Works** (R5–R6), **IV The Belfry** (R7–R9). Each room shows its goal at the top right with
one pip per step (named once done) and a hollow diamond for its optional page.

Every solution below is also played automatically by `tests/chapter_suite.gd`
(scenario names in brackets), using the player's real movement and input.

## Rules the whole chapter follows

- **Power:** plates, levers, rune switches, braziers, crystals and the seal are *sources*;
  gates, lenses, bridges, traps are *consumers*. A carved violet groove on the wall joins
  each source to what it powers and glows while powered. Matching end symbols
  (◇ ○ △ ✚) mark linked pieces.
- **Held, not latched:** a gate worked by plates or crystals is open only while they are
  held down or lit; take the block off or turn the light away and it closes again. Once
  you walk through it into the next room, that room is saved as solved and its gate stays
  open behind you (also after a death). The Room 8 levers are the one exception: the loft
  gate stays open once both are down together.
- **Magic light:** beams (from braziers and lenses) travel straight, turn 90° off a
  mirror's silver face, are stopped by its dark back, and stop at the first solid thing
  (walls, blocks, gates, frozen gargoyles). Auras (wisps, braziers with a radius) reach
  anything within their radius that they can see. Decorative wall torches never count.
- **Room reset** (Pause → Map → Reset Room): puts the current room's puzzle back to its start and
  returns the player to the room entrance. Health is **not** restored. Echoes and
  projectiles in flight are cleared. Saved progress (solved gates, pulled shortcut levers,
  collected pages) is kept.
- **Death** (health reaches 0): the death screen, then a restart at the last lit shrine.
  Solved rooms stay solved. Spike hits that don't kill are *not* deaths: they return you to
  the last lit candle with the damage taken.
- **Mana is never a lock:** anything a fireball does can be done by reloading mana (R)
  first; bells can also be rung by their rope.
- **Jumps:** a jump leaves the ground the moment it is pressed (with a 0.1 s grace after
  running off a ledge). The high jump is gathered by holding **crouch** (S / Down): after
  1 s it starts to count, full at 2.5 s, then press jump. Heights are unchanged: about
  62 px tapped, about 132 px fully charged.
- **Pistons** now work like spikes: a crush costs 2 health and fades you back to the
  piston's candle (or the last safe ground before it).
- **Echoes** repeat everything recorded, including pulling levers, ringing bell ropes and
  turning mirror wheels (not hint stones or the great bell). Holding E lets every Echo fade.
- **Guidance** (Pause → Settings → Gameplay): Full shows the goal card, lights the groove
  when you stand near a switch, shows a faint key cue near things you can use, and makes a
  room's hint stone call out after 90 s without progress or two deaths there. Light keeps
  only the goal card; Off shows none of it.

## Room 1 — The Forgotten Entrance  [r1]

- **Teaches:** pushing blocks, plates, following a groove to its gate.
- **Solution:** walk into the stone block and keep walking. It slides right until the
  stone kerb stops it — exactly on the plate. The gate at the far end is open for as long
  as the block rests there.
- **Why it can't soft-lock:** the kerb makes it impossible to push the block past the plate.
- **Reset:** block returns to its start and the gate closes, unless you have already walked
  through it into Room 2 (then the room is saved as solved and the gate stays open).
- **Optional page (i):** on the +96 ledge at the far left — needs a charged jump
  (hold crouch about 2 s, then jump). A tapped jump can't reach it.
- **Start shrine:** `ch1.cp.start`.

## Room 2 — The Bellkeeper's Cell  [r2]

- **Teaches:** Bellbound Slimes — they hop to a rung bell and sit there for 12 s.
- **Setup:** a slime lives in a cell under an iron grate you walk over. Plate A is in the
  cell under a bell; plate B is on the main floor, with a stone block beside the cell and a
  +32 kerb just past the plate. The gate is open only **while both plates are held** (after
  0.5 s); it closes again when either is let go.
- **Solution:** walk over the grate into the block and keep walking: the kerb stops it on
  plate B. Pull the bell's rope (W at the rope end), then hop over the block and walk
  through the gate while the slime sits on plate A.
- **Once through:** walking into Room 3 saves the room as solved; the gate stays open behind
  you from then on.
- **Alternative (tested):** stand on plate B yourself and hit the bell with a fireball (the
  gate opens, but closes when you step off, so the block is the way through).
- **Alternative (not tested):** record an Echo pulling the rope, or standing on B.
- **Failure states:** none any more. Fireballs can't start below the grate and splash on
  its bars, and the caged slime is warded (a hit only makes its ward shimmer), so it can't
  be killed by accident. (Reset Room would still revive it.)
- **Optional page (ii):** on the +100 ledge above plate B (charged jump).

## Room 3 — The Split Flame  [r3]

- **Teaches:** braziers, beams, mirrors, crystals; two stages from one flame.
- **Shrine:** `ch1.cp.r3` at the entrance.
- **Solution:**
  1. Fireball the brazier. Its beam runs right and stops on the low mirror's dark back.
  2. Turn the low mirror (W at its post) twice: the beam climbs to the hanging mirror, which
     sends it left into the crystal on the left wall. Spectral stairs appear.
  3. Climb the stairs (tapped jumps) to the gallery. Turn the high mirror from the wheel on
     the gallery (three turns) until its beam runs right, under the gallery, into the crystal
     on the right wall. The exit gate opens.
  4. Walk back down; the exit is on the floor under the gallery.
- **Light must stay on:** the gate is open only while the beam rests on the right crystal;
  turn the mirror away and it closes. The stairs stand while either crystal is lit, so the
  way back up to the gallery never disappears once the gate is open.
- **Reset:** mirrors return to their start angles and the brazier goes out; stairs and gate
  go too, unless you have already walked on into Room 4.
- **Optional page (iii):** on the gallery.

## Room 4 — The Silent Watcher  [r4]

- **Teaches:** Watcher Gargoyles — active in darkness, stone in magic light; stone is heavy.
- **Setup:** a lever near the entrance opens a lens in the ceiling; its beam drops onto a
  fixed mirror and sweeps across the whole hall at gargoyle height. The heavy plate (marked
  with carved blocks) is pressed only by stone: pushable blocks or frozen gargoyles — never
  the player or an Echo.
- **Solution:** wait at the lever (the watcher can't see you there) and pull it just as the
  watcher walks onto the heavy plate. It freezes there and the gate opens; it stays open
  while the lens stays open and the watcher stays stone on the plate.
- **Wrong moment:** it freezes wherever it is. Pull the lever again to release it (it stirs
  harmlessly for a second) and wait for its next pass.
- **Danger:** walk into its territory while it is active and it winds up (red eyes flare)
  and strikes for 2 damage.
- **Crossing:** hop over the frozen watcher; you can stand on its back.
- **Optional page (iv):** on the high ledge (+128). Easy with a charged jump from the frozen
  watcher's back; from the floor only a perfectly charged jump reaches it.

## Room 5 — Broken Machinery  [r5]

- **Shrine:** `ch1.cp.r5` at the entrance.
- **Route:** ride the lift up to the works → cross the rising spikes while they are down
  (a candle catches non-lethal hits) → run under each piston while it is raised (a crush
  fades you back to the piston candle just before them) → push
  the stone block ahead of you all the way to the wall under the skull plaque (the plaque is set into the wall; the block stops every bolt) →
  stand on the block and hop up to the ledge → pass the two vents between bursts (they
  flare before bursting) → drop into the end hall.
- **Shortcut:** the one-way lever in the end hall opens the tunnel gate under the works,
  straight back to the lift. It stays open forever (saved), including after a reload.
- **Optional page (v):** inside the tunnel.
- **Reset:** hazards restart their cycles, the block and lift return; the shortcut stays.

## Room 6 — Lantern Crossing  [r6]

- **Teaches:** Lantern Wisps; platforms revealed by light; crumbling ledges; light bridges.
- **Shrine:** `ch1.cp.r6` at the entrance (a death here no longer replays Room 5).
- **Setup:** a spiked chasm (non-lethal: candle recovery). Faint dotted outlines mark hidden
  platforms that are solid only in the wisp's light.
- **Solution:**
  1. The wisp starts at the first bracket and lights the first platform. Hop on.
  2. On each platform, stand on its rune stone and call the wisp (W) to the next bracket.
     The platform you stand on stays lit while the next one lights up. Hop across.
     Repeat to the island (candle).
  3. From the island, call the wisp to the far bracket by the crystal. The crystal raises
     the light bridge over the last chasm.
  4. Cross the two cracked ledges without stopping (each holds for 0.7 s), then the bridge.
- **Return shortcut:** the one-way lever past the bridge raises a glowing walkway over all
  three chasms (saved).
- **Failure states:** falling in returns you to the last candle; call the wisp back from the
  rune stone at the entrance if it is ahead of you.
- **Optional page (vi):** floating above the second cracked ledge — jump for it.

## Room 7 — The Sealed Belfry  [r7]

- **Shrine:** `ch1.cp.finale` at the entrance.
- **Setup:** a flame on a wall shelf aims its beam at a barred window above the doorway in a
  stone partition. Past it hangs a mirror over the heavy plate in the watcher's territory.
  A slime sits in a grated cell under a bell; the plate in the cell holds the window open.
- **Solution:**
  1. Fireball the belfry flame. Its beam stops at the closed window.
  2. Walk through the partition to the mirror's wheel (outside the watcher's territory) and
     turn it twice, so it faces down toward the heavy plate. The camera shows it turning.
  3. Go back and ring the bell. The slime sits on its plate (12 s), the window opens, light
     reaches the mirror and falls on the heavy plate.
  4. When the watcher walks into the light it turns to stone on the heavy plate. The seal
     over the great door wakes rune by rune, the bell tolls, and the door opens.
  5. The solved seal keeps the window open, so the watcher stays stone. Hop over it and go
     through the great door, past the chamber, into the Choir Loft.
- **Alternatives:** the bell by fireball, or an Echo pulling the bell rope (untested).
- **Failure states:** freeze nothing in time → ring again, or Reset Room. (The caged slime
  is warded, as in Room 2.)
- **Optional page (vii):** in the chamber beyond the great door.

## Room 8 — The Choir Loft  [r8]

- **Teaches:** Echoes use things too (levers, ropes, mirror wheels); spring levers.
- **Setup:** two spring levers 768 px apart. A spring lever snaps back up 2.5 s after it is
  pulled (a shrinking ring on its handle shows the time left). The loft gate opens, and
  stays open, only while both are down at the same moment. Sprinting from one to the other
  takes about 3.5 s, so it can't be done alone (tested).
- **Solution:** at the first lever, start recording (E), wait a couple of seconds, pull the
  lever, stop recording (E). Sprint to the second lever. When your Echo pulls the first
  lever again, pull the second.

## Room 9 — The Great Bell  [r9] (finale)

- **Teaches:** Echoes as a second pair of hands, one last time.
- **Setup:** the great bell hangs over a high balcony. Spectral stairs rise to it only while
  the plate at the foot of the tower is held; stepping off lowers them (tested).
- **Solution:** record an Echo walking onto the plate and stop. When it finishes it stays
  on the plate, holding it: climb the stairs (tapped jumps) to the balcony and pull the
  great bell's rope.
- **Ending:** the bell tolls three times, the belfry fills with light, the ledger's last
  line appears (a longer one with all seven pages), then the Chapter Complete card.
  "Keep exploring" stays in the chapter; "Return to Title" goes back to the menu.
