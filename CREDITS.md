# Credits

## Team
- Game, design, code and Echidna's pixel art: the compProject team (see the git history;
  please fill in names and roles).

## Fonts
- **Orbitron** by Matt McInerney, used for the interface (`assets/Text/`). Licensed under
  the SIL Open Font License 1.1. *To do before sharing a build:* add the licence text
  (`OFL.txt`, from the font's release) next to the font files.
- Titles use a serif font installed on the player's computer (Cinzel, Trajan Pro, Times
  New Roman or Georgia, whichever is found first); none is bundled.

## Art
- Dungeon tiles, walls, platforms and props (`assets/TileSet/`): *source to be recorded
  here* (please add where they came from and their licence).
- Parallax fog, ruins, vines, light shafts and the game icon: drawn by scripts in
  `tools/` (no third-party art).

## Audio
- Every sound effect and all the music are synthesised by `tools/generate_sfx.gd` and
  `tools/generate_music.gd` from sine waves and filtered noise; no third-party audio.

## Engine
- Made with [Godot Engine](https://godotengine.org) 4.7.2 (MIT licence).
